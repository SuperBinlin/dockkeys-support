# DockKeys Privacy Policy

DockKeys uses your Mac’s Dock preferences to determine the order of pinned apps. Shortcut mappings, app references, and interface preferences are stored on your Mac. When you select an app for a custom shortcut, DockKeys may store a local reference so it can open that app again.

DockKeys does not require an account or send your Dock contents or shortcut settings to the developer. The app does not include advertising or analytics. The quick-start video is bundled with the app.

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and any information you choose to include and uses it to respond to your request. For questions about privacy or requests concerning information you shared with support, email [superbinlin@163.com](mailto:superbinlin@163.com).
