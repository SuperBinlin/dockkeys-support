Open Dock apps with a keystroke.

Press a shortcut to open or switch to an app. Press it again to hide it. DockKeys matches your shortcuts to the order of apps in your Mac Dock.

Free for Apple silicon Macs · macOS 15.2 or later

Command + 1 opens Studio

The shortcuts match the Dock you already know.

Up to 11 Dock positions

Start with the key to the left of 1, then use 1 through 0. DockKeys shows each shortcut under its app. Move an app in the Dock and its shortcut follows the new position.

Choose your modifier keys

Hold Command or choose a combination of Command, Option, Control, and Shift. Turn off individual Dock shortcuts whenever you need to.

Give any app its own shortcut

Assign a custom shortcut to a favorite app, including apps outside your Dock.
