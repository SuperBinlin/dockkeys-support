# DockKeys Support

DockKeys opens, switches to, and hides Mac apps with keyboard shortcuts. It requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 15.2 or later.

## Get started

1. Open DockKeys and enable Shortcuts.
2. On the Dock Shortcuts page, choose the modifier keys to hold.
3. Use the shortcut shown beneath an app icon. Press it again while the app is active to hide it.

Shortcuts follow the order of pinned apps in your Dock. You can disable a shortcut beneath its icon or assign one separately on the Custom Shortcuts page.

## Get help

If a shortcut is already in use, try a different modifier combination. For other issues, email [superbinlin@163.com](mailto:superbinlin@163.com) with your macOS version, the shortcut involved, and the steps to reproduce the issue.
